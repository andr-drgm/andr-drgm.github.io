**The exchange-rate app** is from high school: live currency conversion, a converter, and a rate
chart. It's still deployed, and I keep it that way on purpose. It's the first thing I built that
a stranger could use without me standing next to them, which is a line worth remembering.

Before the rest of the work here there were also two shorter engagements worth naming.
**Telemed** was an appointment-booking platform for US clinics, where I built the mobile apps and
the backend and wired up Docusign so intake paperwork stopped being an email thread. And an
**SAP ABAP internship** at NTT DATA, building gas-station management software, which is where I
learned what enterprise software costs to change.
